Chapter 8

“I’m not swapping back,” Grant said finally, his voice gentle but firm. “This is my life now.”

He picked up his makeup mirror, slipped it into his purse, and walked away, leaving Sarah standing alone among the trees. The skirt swayed softly around his legs as he headed home.

Chapter 9

In the weeks that followed, Grant settled more deeply into his new life as Mrs. Erik Pampel. He often found himself reflecting on the change while doing everyday things-making coffee in the morning, shopping for groceries, or getting ready for the day.

To his quiet surprise, being a woman wasn’t as radically different as he had once imagined it would be. Chapter 10

Grant nodded, resting his head against Erik’s chest. “Man and wife.”

The truth was out, the air was clear, and their future-however unexpected-felt solid once more.

Chapter 11

As Erik and Grant left the apartment, they walked in silence for a while. The person they had just visited was unmistakably Sarah-inside and out-despite the male body she currently wore. She had reclaimed her identity with fierce determination.

Grant slipped her hand into Erik’s as they walked back to the car. Life had taken them all in completely unexpected directions, but each of them was finding their own way forward.

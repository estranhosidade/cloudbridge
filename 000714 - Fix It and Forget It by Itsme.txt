“No, I'll make you all the girl's friends. Then it'll be like their own has the part. They won't be mean to you anymore.”

Dominick wasn't sure if he wanted a bunch of seven-year-olds to be his friends. But before Dominick could protest, Laura used her power again. She made all of Connie's friends become Dominick's friends too. But it extended to more than that. Dominick didn't realize it, but back at home, he wasn't friends with any of his old friends. All of his friends were little girls

Chapter 12:
When Dominick returned to dance class, he had become just one of the students. He was friendly with everyone. They were all excited to have such a good dancer to lead them at the recital. Even Maggie was excited to have Dominick in the lead. She knew she couldn't dance as well and with the pressure of being the lead gone, Maggie was happier too.

At ice cream, Dominick was treated like everyone else. There was one exception, one of the girls was having a birthday party, she was turning eight, but Dominick wasn't invited. Her mother didn't think it was appropriate to invite a man Dominick's age to a party full of little girls.

When Laura found out what had happened, she used her magic to make Dominick a young girl like all the rest of her friends. Dommie became the youngest girl in the class.

“Dommie, your mommy is on the phone,” Laura informed her.

“Hello, Mommy.”

“Hello, Sweetheart. Having fun at Aunt Laura's?”

“Yeah, it's been loads of fun.”

“Great. I have some wonderful news for you. I'm coming tomorrow to take you home.” Since Dommie wasn't a teenage boy with those nasty friends, she wasn't in trouble anymore. That bit of history had been removed from reality. There wasn't any reason for her to not be back at home.

“Do I have to come home now?”

“What's wrong?”

“I've been practicing for the recital. I'm the star of the show.”

“You are?”

“Yeah.”

“Aunt Laura didn't tell me.”

“Well, I am. I have to finish.”

“When is the show?”

“Two weeks on Saturday.”

“I guess I can wait another two weeks.”

“Will you come to my show?”

“I wouldn't miss it.”

“Yay!”

Two weeks later, Laura and Dommie's mommy sat in the front row at the recital. The girls were doing a marvelous job. Everyone enjoyed the performance. Laura kept an eye on all the dancers, she knew that not all of them were as they seemed. Dommie wasn't the only little girl who had been something else. Connie used to be Conrad, Laura's husband, and Dominick's uncle. Sarah used to be Laura's former best friend and Conrad's mistress. Now they were innocent little girls dancing their hearts out.

See a family of three
Walking through the mall, you saw a family of three: a mother, father and daughter of about 14.

The daughter was paused at a window of a clothing store. She was admiring some of the outfits displayed there. The girl entered the store and her parents followed her in. You decided to tag along

You used the orb on the parents first, their conversation changed without them being aware of it. As they watched their daughter looking through the trendy apparel, the father said, "I thought you said he would grow out of it."

"I thought he would," the mother replied.

"It's worse than ever. We shouldn't have indulged him."

"I think you are right."

"This has got to stop. He is becoming a man, he has to stop dressing like that and looking like that and all the rest of the stuff."

"I thought it was just a phase."

"It's not. We have to put an end to it right now."

"We can't just do it all at once. I think it has to be gradual."

"I suppose, but we are starting today."

"Yeah, you're right."

"I think the first thing we should do is get him a haircut. His hair is down to his shoulders...."

Bobbie, their daughter was unaware of what her parents were talking about. She was blissfully looking at the newest stuff and hoping the new fashions would look good on her. You used the orb on her to make sure that she could never complain to her parents no matter what they did.

Bobbie showed her parents what she had picked out and hoped they would buy them for her.

"I don't think so," her father said doubtfully. "Not now anyway." Neither her father nor her mother would buy Bobbie any clothes that wasn't from the boys or mens department again.

"Bobby," her dad said, "let's get you a haircut."

Bobbie didn't mind the idea too much. That was until she was taken to a unisex barber. But Bobby didn't complain.

Her father talked to the barber/stylist privately. Bobbie hated as she saw the stylist cutting off all her long beautiful hair. She couldn't complain in front of her father though. She had to grin and bare it.

Her long hair was cut off. It wasn't made extremely short (by a boy's standard) but it was cut such that the longest strands were now hardly two inches long and styled in a boyish cut.

Bobbie hated the look, but she couldn't argue with her parents. She just couldn't.

It would take many months, but eventually Bobbie's parents would make a man out of her.


Bobbie's/Bobby's perspective
The next day, Bobbie returned to school.

"What did you do to your hair?" Bobbie's friend Cathy asked.

"Mom and Dad took me to get my hair cut and this is how it turned out."

"It's so short."

"Don't you think I know that."

Bobbie borrowed a brush from Cathy and restyled her hair into a more feminine style. She also borrowed a bit of makeup too after she explained that her parents didn't want her to use makeup anymore. Bobbie fixed her hair and her face, but she knew she had to remove all her 'improvements' before going home.

Meanwhile, Bobbie's mother removed all of Bobbie's panties which weren't made out of cotton (such as nylon and microfiber) and threw them out. She also started to slowly pair down Bobbie's bottoms by removing some of her skirts. It would take time before Bobbie's mother started to buy unisex/male clothing for her daughter, but this was a start.

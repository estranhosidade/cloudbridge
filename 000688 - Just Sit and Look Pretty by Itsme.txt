The parents refused to admit all wrongdoing They gave a different story entirely. They said that the swap was only for a day, but things got out of hand. Regardless, mom and dad lost custody of the children and weren't to have any contact with them ever again.

For the first day, Johnny would be driven to school. Tomorrow he would take the bus with the rest of the children.

Johnny had to ride in the back of the car just like any other toddler. It didn't matter how old he was mentally, it was a matter of safety. Children his size had to sit in a safety seat in the rear. It was the law.

Inside the garage, Johnny could see what was supposed to be his mountain bike. He was too small to ride it. His foster parents put training wheels on the bike. Johnny was annoyed that Gracie was using his bike to learn how to ride. Sometimes, he watched her on his bike from his window. It wasn't fair. She got to use his mountain bike during the day, and he got to use her training pants at night. His new muscles weren't strong enough to keep him from wetting the bed while he was sleeping.

The school looked twice the size to Johnny as it had been since Johnny had been here last. He would attend the same classes as before, but special small desks were brought in to accommodate him. His seat would also be at the front of the class to prevent the other kids from blocking his view.

Johnny entered his first-period class. The teacher knew it was him immediately. Even though she had never seen Johnny in his new body, it was obvious. He was the only three-year-old in the ninth grade.

Johnny walked into the room tentatively. He looked around for his friends. He saw several of them. Everyone looked over the 'small girl' who was entering their classroom.

“The Do Now is on the board. Get to your work!” the teacher announced.

“Welcome back, Johnny. We've missed you,” the teacher told him.

“Hello, Mrs. Crabapple.”

“Your seat is right there,” she said pointing to the small desk.

Johnny sat in his chair. He took out a small notebook and wrote down his assignment. He was a week behind. He hadn't seen this type of problem before. Since he couldn't do the problem, he perused the room again. He saw the stares from some of the other students.

After the class ended, a couple of the kids surrounded him.

“Johnny, is it really you?”

Johnny nodded.

“How does it feel?”

“I don't know how to describe it. Everything looks much bigger now.”

“Is it true, you're a girl?”

Dressed in male apparel with a short haircut, it was impossible to say if the three-year-old was a boy or a girl. Johnny nodded again.

"Are you wearing diapers?"

"No, are you!" Johnny answered indignantly.

"Are you going to swap back?"

"I hope so?"

"When?'

"I don't know."

“I don't believe it.”

“I don't know what to say.”

“You had better hurry to your next class. The bell will ring soon,” the teacher reminded her students.

The group exited the classroom. The questions continued. Johnny didn't have any real answers or answers he wanted to say.

End.

An Ordinary Family by Itsme

Matthew was texted from his nephew Dylan to come over one Saturday afternoon. Dylan was going to take an important state-wide math test and he wanted Matthew's help. Matthew had been a math teacher years ago and still knew his math backwards and forwards. But in recent years he had made plenty of money through the Internet. He had made so much money that he quit is job as a teacher and never looked back. Being that this was one of his favorite nephews, Matthew dropped what he was doing and came right over.

On the way, Matthew fretted about the effectiveness of his efforts to teach the boy. Dylan wasn't the best student and he should have called Matthew months ago. Not a few days before the test. Maybe all he needed was just a little tune-up and some test taking techniques to improve his score Matthew hoped. Regardless, it didn't take more than a half hour to arrive at Dylan's house.

Matthew rang the door bell and eighteen year old Dylan answered the door quickly. Matthew liked that Dylan was ready for him. He didn't want to waste time as teenagers often do. Dylan and Matthew walked down the hall passing Annie's bedroom. Annie was Dylan's six year old little sister. Matthew saw into her room, Annie was playing with Jerry, her father and Dylan's step-father. They were sitting across from each other at a table with stuffed animals on the other other sides. They were playing some sort of card game with cards in the middle and other cards in front of the stuffed animals as it the animals were playing too.

Father and daughter playing together was heartwarming to see. They were both laughing and giggling as they threw cards in to the middle to a game that no one really knew the rules.

Matthew caught sight of his sister Phillis who was cleaning Julie's room. The room was a mess, she was still trying to pick up everything off the floor, before she started vacuuming.

Twelve year old Julie was Phillis's other girl. Phillis had three children, Dylan, Julie and Annie. Julie and Annie were from her second marriage, Dylan was the oldest from Phillis's first marriage.

Phillis was dressed in her cleaning attire, a pair of leggings (she loved to wear leggings, just like her older daughter) and a loose fitting t-shirt in a pinkish color and some sort of slogan on the front. She passed by so quickly that Matthew didn't have a chance to read it. Phillis did say a brief “hey” as she continued cleaning Julie's room.

Matthew sat down with Dylan and as he expected, Dylan wasn't ready for his test. He didn't need an afternoon, he needed weeks or months to be ready for a heady exam like this. Matthew did what he could but it was truly hopeless. Sometimes it seemed that Dylan belonged in the seventh grade, not a graduating senior. It was a good thing that Dylan didn't have to pass this test to graduate. There was only a month and a half left of school. Matthew reasoned that Dylan was just coasting as his graduation was assured.

Matthew spend an uneventful time at the house. Dylan was trying hard, but it didn't seem like he was making much progress. Matthew doubted he would pass the test. Phillis finished cleaning Julie's room. Now she was taking her clothes to the washer and dryer to be cleaned. Of course, some of her clothes had to be hand washed too. Annie and Jerry stopped playing for a while to have a snack and a juice box. Then they returned to Annie's room to play again.

Matthew heard a car pull up into the driveway, and then a moment later, the door next to the garage opened. It was Julie, she was carrying some packages. She returned to her now clean room and closed the door.

After several hours, Matthew was getting frustrated with Dylan's lack of progress. His voice was raised in exasperation as he showed Dylan the same procedure to solving a problem for the third or fourth time. He didn't notice that Julie had entered the room.

“You think this is so easy don't you?” she told Matthew.

“It should be,” Matthew retorted.

“I'm sure he'll do better if he studies alone. Come with me and I'll show you something really hard.” Matthew didn't know it, but everything he knew about math was swapped into Dylan's mind with the seventh grade math knowledge that Dylan had formerly possessed. Up until this point, Dylan had had Julie's seventh grade education and she had his.

Matthew initially didn't want to come with Julie, but he quickly changed his mind. He followed Julie to her parent's bedroom. “School is easy, picking out an outfit is hard. You should try it.”

“What?”

“You heard me, take off what you are wearing and pick out something nice.”

Julie left the room and closed the door. Matthew wanted to leave, but instead, he started to strip off all his clothes. He carefully folded his clothes and left them on top of the dresser. Then completely naked he looked through the closets for something nice to wear. He ignored Jerry's clothing and concentrated only on Phillis's. Not wanting to be naked anymore he put on the first pair of panties he found. It was a pair of bikini briefs in a teal color. Next he put on a black sports bra. The sports bra hugged his hairy chest. It barely jutted out as it had some padding. He put a sleeveless top over the bra, which looked ridiculous, especially because of his hairy armpits. Also the top was too light in color for the black bra underneath. Finally he found a pair of ankle length leggings and a pair of flats. The colors weren't hideous together, but they didn't exactly match either. When Julie returned to the room, Matthew was sitting in front of Phillis's vanity trying to put on makeup.

“Mom, come here. You have to see this.”

“I'm about to start dinner.”

“Mom come here now!” Julie demanded.

A couple seconds later, Phillis was also in the doorway seeing Matthew in her clothes and trying to put on her makeup.

“What are you doing in my clothes?” Phillis asked.

“Mom, you know you should share. But I do think he needs some pointers. Why don't you teach him how to put on a proper outfit?”

“I can't I have to make dinner.”

“Don't worry about that. I'll have Annie do it.” As Phillis headed towards Matthew to get him into some proper clothes, something nice and cute from her wardrobe, all of her knowledge about cooking exited her mind and entered Annie's.

Julie went to Annie's room, she was still playing with Jerry. “Annie, can you make dinner, Mom is busy helping Uncle Matthew.”

“OK,” six year old Annie said as she not only knew what to make, but what ingredients she would need. “But I need to pick up some things from the grocery store.”

“I'll get them, give me a list of what you want.”

Julie didn't mind driving, it hadn't been that long before she removed that knowledge from Phillis's mind so she could drive for herself.

“I wanna go shopping,” Jerry said enthusiastically as he put down the doll he was playing with.

“Will you be good at the store?”

“Yeah.”

“...and not beg for candy?”

“Yeah.”

“Promise?”

“I promise.”

“OK, you can come then.”

Annie wrote out a list for Julie. Jerry was trailing right behind her. He was worried that Julie would leave without him. But before they left, Jerry said, wait one minute and he scampered off. Only to return with a teddy bear in his arms. “I couldn't forget Mr. Giggles,” he announced.

Julie buckled Jerry into the back seat and then they drove off to fetch the groceries.
